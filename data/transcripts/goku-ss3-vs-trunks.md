# Red SS3 Goku (FB11) vs Future Trunks: lessons
- Don't defend every swing early; Goku awakens at low life, so 3-4 life is acceptable. Fear the 5-cost Double Strike.
- Charge an early copy of a late-game 4-of finisher.
- Prefer the 7-cost body: the opponent struggles to remove it on their turn, while 4- and 5-costs get bounced.
- Bulma's -5000 / -10000 can split across targets; pair with God Kamehameha (KO 30000 or less) to remove big bodies.
- It's Over! (with a marker) to wipe a wide board when attacking into it can't win.
- Poke at 3 life to make the opponent defend and spend cards, then finish once their hand is small.
