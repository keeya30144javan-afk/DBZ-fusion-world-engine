# Deck profile: Yellow Gogeta (Son Goku/Vegeta FB09-073) by Hamza (god rank), Hawk Dragon Ball Nest: key points
- 10-12 cantrips; ST01-052 Vegeta cantrip brought the deck back.
- Leader back side: rest an Another World card, untap another, giving extra swings with Gogeta FB09-122 or the 5-cost Goku.
- Kill turn: hold the Leader swing to the end.
- Vs SS3: Vegeta cantrip, Majin Vegeta 30k, then double Warrior clears 3 bodies, staying hand neutral.
- Gogeta 6-drop is the crutch (goes to life instead of dying); only blue answers it cleanly.
- Tech slots: promo Vegeta FP-073 (blue Vegeta/Broly/Vegito matchups), Blue Power Awakening vs blue Vegito, West/South Kai, barrier Vegeta vs red BR.
- Weakness: needs combo pieces; the 2-cost duo on curve going first is mandatory vs Majin Vegeta/Beast Gohan.
