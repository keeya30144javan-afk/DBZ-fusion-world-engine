# Semifinal (Bo3): SS3 Goku (Bourja) vs Gogeta: BR (Zeus): lessons
- Open decklist: weigh which techs the opponent might run; avoid lines one tech ruins.
- Babidi [Barrier] turns off Gogeta's 1-life win: play it once they commit to FB09-121, not early.
- Sphere of Destruction at exactly 4 life vs SS3 makes them awaken at 2 (the Leader takes a life on awaken).
- Gotenks FS11-03 locks a rested Gogeta so it can't attack; huge before the Gogeta turn.
- Results of Training was charged and later cost game 2 by 5k: its +15k and draw needed a rested cost 3+ card.
- Super Combo to protect Vegeta: BR is wasted; its own Activate KOs itself.
- Hand sizes and Super Combos in the Drop decided all three games (5k margins). 30k swings force 2 cards or a Super Combo.
- Leader swing first (free), then commit; don't overpush a low-life opponent if it fuels their counterattack.
