# Tournament: yellow SS3 Goku mirror (Kong Wilson vs Bourja): lessons
- The front Leader takes a life each end of turn, so it awakens about turn 3 on schedule. The back side untaps 2 energy when you play a 20k-30k [Evolve] card (playing one counts; it doesn't need to evolve) with 3+ rested energy.
- Charge to 4 and stop; the Leader supplies the extra energy.
- Bread and butter: 1-cost Goku, evolve into SS3 and get 2 energy back, swing 30k, untap and swing again.
- Aggressive pace: both players at 3-4 life by minute 5.
- Both players went to 1 life; the one with the bigger hand (11-12 cards) and Kamehameha + Blue Power Awakening pushed ~150k. The defender was 20k short with only one Super Combo.
- Babidi [Barrier] tech beats Gogeta: BR's alternate win.
