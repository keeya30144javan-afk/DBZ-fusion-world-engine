# Tournament final: Gogeta: BR (Persiki) vs Black Son Goku / Namek (Shiva Master): lessons
- Gogeta: BR wins with FB09-121 at end of turn when the opponent is at 1 life with no Battle Cards; going to 1 life vs it is fatal.
- Gogeta's last-turn push: go down to 1 yourself for more cards, force Super Combos with 30k swings, then fuse and wipe.
- Namek deck: Ki is close to a card. Spend battle-card Ki first, use the Leader's once-per-turn Ki combo to defend for free, and save a reserve for the final push.
- Board control against fusion decks forces them to rebuild their bases instead of fusing.
- 30k swings are the magic number: they force 2 cards or a Super Combo.
- Count Super Combos in the opponent's Drop before going all-in.
- Late game, prefer cards in hand over energy when there's nothing big left to cast.
