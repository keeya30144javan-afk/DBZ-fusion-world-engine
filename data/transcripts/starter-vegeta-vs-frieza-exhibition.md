# Exhibition: blue Vegeta starter (Michael Carrick) vs yellow Frieza starter (David Fujimaru): lessons
- Ties go to the attacker; a 100k all-in vs 100k of combos tied, and the attacker won.
- Don't hit an opponent's Leader into a strong awaken; attack and rest their bodies instead.
- Use blockers while they still have value; yellow rests them anyway.
- Awakened Frieza untaps 2 Battle Cards at end of turn, so its blockers can attack too.
- Charging isn't mandatory; keep cards in hand when energy isn't needed.
- Blue Vegeta: get to 7 or fewer cards through useful combos, not wasted ones.
