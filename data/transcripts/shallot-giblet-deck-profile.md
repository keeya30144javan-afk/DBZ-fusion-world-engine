# Deck profile: Shallot/Giblet (yellow, FB11) by Hawk Dragon Ball Nest: key points
- Plan: hand-neutral Shallots (like SS3) + Fusion Evolve every turn (like BR). 10 Shallot / 10 Giblet.
- Leader back side (awakens at 5): untap 2 energy if Shallot and Giblet are in play, but then no Battle Cards with original cost ≤4 that turn, so sequence the 2-costs first.
- Searchers only hit Saiyan-only Battle Cards cost 2-6: no Extras or Super Combos.
- FB11-086 Shallot: never charge or combo. FB11-085: first to combo.
- Shallet (6-cost, 35k/40k on your turn): Fusion Evolve over rested Shallot + Giblet with equal power. When attacking, pay 1 to untap; if played this turn, also untap the Leader or a card.
- Swing Leader first; don't tunnel-vision: untap a 5-cost Shallot (draw + rest energy) or swing a second 40k for game.
- Weaknesses: low power outside Shallet (25k evolves); Giblets go minus in hand; deciding what to combo/charge.
- Cut: promo Shallot (-1 in hand), Vegito (-3 in hand), 5-cost Goku (KO effect rarely fires).
