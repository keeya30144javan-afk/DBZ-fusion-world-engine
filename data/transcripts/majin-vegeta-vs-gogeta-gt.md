# Majin Vegeta (Young Bully's god-rank list) vs Gogeta GT, Hawk Dragon Ball Nest: lessons
- Piccolo/Nail adds early aggro (15k then 25k swings for 2 energy) beyond Krillin.
- Going second is good: attack on turn 1 and keep the marker for flexibility; don't pop it on turn 1 just for two Krillins.
- GT defended every swing early and sat at 2-4 cards in hand, so it could never kill. Take early hits instead.
- Missed a lethal by not comboing to 30k; at a 2-card opponent hand, check lethal every turn.
- Play the combo-denial card before the swing it protects.
- Wicked Soul Discovered: Ki each turn and cycling find the pieces; the 0-cost rare Vegeta wants Ki a turn earlier.
