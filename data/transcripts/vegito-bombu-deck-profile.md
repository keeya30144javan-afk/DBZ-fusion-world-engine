# Deck profile: Vegito (FB05/ST01, Bombu build, 60 cards) by Hawk Dragon Ball Nest: key points
- Turn 1 Pan, turn 2 Gotenks package: 10 in Drop by turn 3. 60 cards to ease charge decisions.
- Don't rush awaken at 4; once at 4 stay there (SS3/BR combo you from 3 to 2 for Double Strike range).
- Recycle Super Combos, Saviors and duos; the late-game deck is 5-8 cards of mostly defence.
- Always check the Drop for every chain piece before starting the chain.
- Win line: SCR midgame, keep their board empty; Bombu package late = too many swings.
- Gotenks vs Majin Vegeta: bounce their 4-cost, stop attacks, keep Saviors live.
- Cut: Goten, 3-cost promo Vegito, anti-lock Vegeta, Piercing Sword of Light (no combo power).
